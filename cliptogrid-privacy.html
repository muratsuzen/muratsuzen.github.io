Privacy Policy for ClipToGrid

Effective Date: June 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy governs the ClipToGrid Chrome extension and explains how data is handled.

1. Data Collection and Usage

ClipToGrid is a 100% client-side application. It utilizes the clipboardRead permission strictly to access text, lists, or Markdown tables that you intentionally copy and request to parse via the "Paste from Clipboard" button. This data is handled entirely in your browser's local volatile memory (RAM) for the sole purpose of rendering a preview grid and generating downloadable Excel (.xlsx) or CSV files.

2. Data Transmission and Storage

We do NOT collect, store, log, or transmit any of your personal data, clipboard contents, or browsing history. No data is ever sent to external servers, third parties, or database systems. Everything happens locally on your device.

3. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official repository: GitHub Repository.
